Team Fortress 2

Discovered shortly after I started playing Garry's Mod. Installed it for the sole purpose of GMod asset mounting. My friends kept yelling at me to actually play it, and I finally did after about a year of having it in my Steam library. Pretty good, and probably the only online competitive shooter game I'll ever get around to playing.

Stardew Valley

Like if Animal Crossing and Minecraft had a baby, which were two of my top games in 2020. I finish a full farm year maybe every six real life months.

Terraria

Started playing this a year after Journey's End after friends introduced me to it. Very addictive, played some modded too.

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

The only game I have ever pre-ordered, and the only game I have ever been really, truly hyped about. I played Breath of the Wild back in 2018-19 on my Wii U, so I finished it up around the same time that TOTK was announced. The wait felt long, but very worth it.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

Proud Ganondorf main, with a Daisy secondary. Yes, Ganondorf. Daisy because it's awesome to just kill people as a princess, and Ganondorf because I picked him up when I played with friends before getting the game and learning actual tier lists, and just kept using him because he's low tier.

HITMAN: World of Assassination

Thanks to watching RTGame and a friend letting me demo this on his PC, I have ended up sinking multiple hours into killing people in stupid ways. It functions as both a stealth and sandbox game with splendid ragdoll physics.

Garry's Mod

Started in 2022, so I probably missed the golden age of machinima and such. Still fun though, also got me into the rest of Valve's Source games.
